# Generate Entries with the model rather than querying published dictionaries

Entries are generated by the same Anthropic call that already produces Translations, not
fetched from oed.com, svenska.se or szotar.sztaki.hu. None of those three publishes a
usable API: OED's is a paid licence with its content behind a subscription login, and the
other two would require scraping HTML written for human readers. Doing so would mean three
separate scrapers with three shapes and three redesign risks, covering three of our six
languages — and, because this repo ships prebuilt binaries, distributing automated access
to those sites rather than reading them once ourselves.

## Considered options

Scraping was weighed on more than convenience. Two of the three sites are in the EU, where
there is no fair-use doctrine — only a closed list of exceptions plus a database right that
specifically forbids repeated extraction of small parts. For OED, the fourth US fair-use
factor is the hard one: its product *is* individual entry lookups sold by subscription, so
substituting the visit substitutes the product. Not retaining the data does not help,
because the harm is in the substituted read.

## Consequences

An Entry is **generated, not cited**. It can be quietly wrong about a rare Sense, an
etymology, or a regional register, and it carries no authority. Two things follow from
this and should not be removed casually: Senses are capped so the model is not pushed into
the long tail where it is least reliable, and every Entry carries Source Links out to the
published dictionaries so a doubtful Entry is one click from an authority.

The upside is that all six languages are covered uniformly today and any language added
later is covered for free.
